Source: [saved core metrics](../../data/scored/proquest/publication/monthly_core.csv), generated by `src/proquest_macro_benchmark.py`. It conditions on saved forecasts and omits model-selection and extraction uncertainty. Stable incremental skill is not established.

## Stability and calibration

Excluding 2007–2009 leaves 372 evaluation months. Macro-only Brier is 0.6164 versus 0.6214 with newspapers; the estimated improvement becomes −0.0050. Log loss is 0.9626 versus 0.9578, so metrics do not all reverse together. This exclusion removes evaluation months, not crisis information from every subsequent training fit.
