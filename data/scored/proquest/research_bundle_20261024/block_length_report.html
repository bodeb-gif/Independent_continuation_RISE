Monthly six-month forecasts have overlapping outcomes. Disjoint six- or twelve-month blocks may leave relevant correlation across boundaries. Newey-West diagnostics retain lagged covariance, but their bandwidths and asymptotic approximations also require assumptions. These checks condition on fitted forecasts; they do not reproduce model selection uncertainty, validate machine labels, or establish a fresh discovery.
