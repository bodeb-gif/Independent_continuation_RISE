What the new modern review shows

The 50 modern candidates cover every year from 2011 through 2026. Thirty-five were included. Among these 35, topic and direction each agree on 29 (82.9%), horizon on 26 (74.3%), and scope on 24 (68.6%). These are conditional label-agreement rates, not prediction accuracy.

Because sampling was stratified by year, the design-weighted inclusion estimate is 73.4%; the unweighted sample fraction is 70.0%. The sample is small and the historical and modern sampling frames differ. No pooled population estimate is asserted.
