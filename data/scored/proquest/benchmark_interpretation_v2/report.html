Benchmark choice and outcome interpretation

Exploratory measurement experiment. All three outcome definitions and all three fixed-direction comparators are retained. No benchmark is selected by observed accuracy. Original labels, model predictions and p-values remain unchanged.

Primary population: verified annual 1900–2010 claims with machine-stated horizons and endpoint outcomes; NBER proxy rows excluded. Each article has equal total weight within each topic/horizon subset. Original endpoint bands are 2% production, 5% stock price, 1.5% CPI and 0.5 unemployment-rate percentage points. Duration scaling multiplies these by months/12; sign only counts any change outside numerical tolerance. Neither alternative is source-validated. All regimes use identical claims.
