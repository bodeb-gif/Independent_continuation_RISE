Why the macro benchmark struggles

Post-hoc audit of the frozen ProQuest forecasts. Core: 1977–2010 (408 months). With CPI: 2002–2010 (108 months). No models were fitted or calibrated for this audit.

Main finding: probability errors on flat outcomes drive the economy-only model's disadvantage. High-confidence forecasts are often wrong, and the short CPI training history produces extreme extrapolation. Training-label revisions occur, but this audit does not identify them as the cause of the forecasting losses.

Feature shifts use that year's saved training mean and scale. We flag |z| > 3 and values outside the training range; neither flag is a normal-distribution assumption, a causal estimate or an automatic exclusion rule.

Concrete example: 2002-09. The economy-only model assigned rise probability 0.99999982, but the revised outcome was flat. The unemployment 12-month change was 1.30 percentage points, 6.45 training standard deviations above its training mean. That feature contributed 13.03 of the 17.34 rise-versus-flat logit margin. This is an exact coefficient decomposition, not a causal explanation of the economy.

Revisions near the ±2% boundary can switch a class even when the change in growth is small. Revisions and moving class proportions complicate learning, but these descriptive counts cannot establish how much loss they caused. No revised training labels have been fed back into the original models.

Implications for the next benchmark

Prioritize a small macro predictor set, stronger regularization candidates and forward-time validation within each training window. Compare expansion/rolling histories only through that same training-only procedure. Assess calibration using earlier out-of-sample predictions; do not recalibrate on these evaluation years. Keep the original base rate and identical evaluation support. Then test the incremental newspaper contribution. The short CPI sample should remain a sensitivity check, with its short history and missing 2001 training class explicit.

These recommendations are motivated by an already inspected sample. A later model comparison on these years remains exploratory, even with proper internal time splits. Human validation, selected-query coverage, quarterly vintage staleness and the broad production target remain separate limitations. No LOC or crisis-window claims are added.

Reproduce and inspect

python src/proquest_macro_diagnostics.py, with the existing information-model dependencies. The source forecasts remain in data/scored/proquest/macro_information/. This folder contains month errors, additive loss decompositions, confidence bins, class probabilities, annual training/evaluation diagnostics, feature shifts, logit contributions, revision rows and transitions, and provenance hashes. All original inputs are checked before and after the audit. No new model or calibration was fitted.
